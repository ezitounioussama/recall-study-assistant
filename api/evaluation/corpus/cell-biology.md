# Cell biology — lecture 3

## Mitochondria
Mitochondria are the organelles that produce ATP through oxidative phosphorylation. They have a double membrane, the inner one folded into cristae, and they carry their own circular DNA, inherited from the mother.

## Photosynthesis
Chloroplasts capture light energy with chlorophyll and use it to fix carbon dioxide into glucose. The light-dependent reactions happen in the thylakoid membranes and release oxygen; the Calvin cycle runs in the stroma.

## Cell membrane
The plasma membrane is a phospholipid bilayer. Small non-polar molecules diffuse through it directly; ions and larger polar molecules need channel or carrier proteins. Osmosis is the diffusion of water across it toward the higher solute concentration.
